# Content creation and promotion
## Use this
- Free camera mode: run-time freecam for cinematic screenshots and trailer shots https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/camera/free-camera [S386]
- Video thumbnails rules: authentic gameplay trailers on the game page (see Thumbnails) [S325]
- Creator Rewards: earn Robux for daily engagement and bringing new or lapsed users https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards [S390]
- Advertising standards: content and disclosure rules for ads https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/comply-with-advertising-standards [S388]
- Brand guidelines update: "On Roblox" badge and avatar use in promotion https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-to-roblox-licensing-program-brand-guidelines/1843674 [S393]
## Concepts
- Capture: use freecam to frame shots; record with your own screen recorder; hide UI for cinematic shots and keep a clean test server with friends or alt accounts for staged scenes [S386].
- Game-page trailer (video thumbnail): must be authentic gameplay without misleading edits; in-game UI text is fine; Roblox catalog music is allowed; added voice-over, narration, voice chat, and music with lyrics are not; overlay text only to describe gameplay; every video is reviewed [S325].
- Video thumbnails appear first on the detail page and can show on Home; not on Xbox, PlayStation, or VR [S325].
- Short-form (TikTok, YouTube Shorts): community advice is to hook in the first 3-5 seconds, shoot vertical 9:16, show the core loop immediately; results differ by platform, so test both (anecdotal) [S396][S395].
- Devlogs mostly attract other developers; player-facing videos (updates, funny moments, challenges) bring players (community opinion) [S395].
- Creator partnerships: small and mid creators matched to your genre; use share links to measure each creator's traffic [S395][S332].
- Creator Rewards (replaced the affiliate program on 2025-07-24): Daily Engagement pays 5 Robux per qualifying user with 10+ minutes in a day under the program's conditions; Audience Expansion pays a 35% revenue share on a new or reactivated user's first $100 of spend, per the framework page [S390][S391].
- Video Stars program members can also earn Creator Rewards [S390].
- Social links on your game page (the only allowed off-platform links) require the owner to be age-checked 16+ [S389][S312].
- Brand use: the "On Roblox" badge can replace the Roblox logo in off-platform promotion without approval for digital marketing; all avatar types can promote your experience; commercial off-platform use of classic R6/R15 avatars needs a license [S393].
- Documenting development: keep a changelog in the repo and Version History publish notes; reuse them for update announcements and devlogs [S368][S49].
- Standout Games nomination asks for gameplay footage, localization, a trailer, and compliant thumbnails; useful checklist even if you never apply [S392].
## Gotchas
- Creator Store music is licensed for Roblox only; it is subject to YouTube/TikTok copyright systems in your videos [S254].
- Ads (including sponsored creator content) must be clearly disclosed and must not mimic Roblox's own ad labels [S388].
- Offering in-game rewards for following or subscribing on external platforms may conflict with Roblox rules (raised by DevForum commenters; ambiguous) [S395].
- The Name and Logo Community Usage Guidelines live on en.help.roblox.com, which was blocked (HTTP 403); read them before using Roblox marks [S394].
- The Creator Affiliate program is deprecated; ignore older guides that rely on it [S391].
- Many "how to market your game" posts are agency pitches; treat numbers and budgets as unverified [S395].
