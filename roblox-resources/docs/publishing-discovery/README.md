## Concepts
- Recommended for You ranks games by signals from players who joined organically from that Home sort; Roblox says the goal is long-term retention, engagement, and monetization, and signals change over time [S323].
- Impressions also move with Roblox algorithm changes, seasonality (weekly peak on Saturday, summer, holidays), and how well competing games engage [S323].
- New games get signals in stages: playthrough and bounce rate (first session), Day 1 (playtime, play days, spend, co-play), Days 2-7, Days 8-28; no 28-day wait [S324].
- A custom thumbnail is required to receive Home recommendation impressions; default-thumbnail games are ineligible [S323].
- Thumbnails: 16:9, ideally 1920x1080; up to 10 images or videos; separate Home page and detail page sets; thumbnail personalization shows each user the most relevant one [S325].
- Video thumbnails show first on the detail page once approved; not supported on Xbox, PlayStation, VR [S325].
- Icon: square, at least 512x512; it is shown scaled down to small sizes, so keep it readable [S326].
- Metadata rules from the discovery guide [S323]:
  - Be accurate; no irrelevant keywords.
  - Use original imagery and names; add your own spin to trends.
  - Metadata implying monetary rewards (giveaways) is not prioritized.
  - Highly mismatched metadata/content is not recommended and ranks lower in search.
  - Games closely resembling existing games (metadata or place files) are deprioritized.
- Genres: set an accurate genre and subgenre; accuracy is checked [S328].
- Descriptions: Roblox publishes experimental guidance for structure and search performance [S327].
- Soft launch: release as friends-only plus Playtest list, or public but opted out of Recommended for You; test retention with a small audience and Experiments before full exposure [S329].
- Paid acquisition: search ads and sponsored campaigns through Ads Manager [S330][S331].
- Share links and referral events attribute and reward invites [S332][S333].
- Reaching players under 16 needs the Kids and Select requirements (see policy-compliance docs) [S314].
## Gotchas
- Limit: 5 never-released games per day; re-releasing a previously released game does not count [S329].
- Do not chase impressions with fake giveaways or clickbait; Roblox deprioritizes both and Community Standards prohibit misleading metadata [S323][S312].
- Retention changes take days to show in recommendation signals because they are computed over Day 1 to Day 28 windows [S324].
- Thumbnail personalization test results (+8.5% average qualified play-through) are Roblox's figures, not guaranteed [S325].
